## My Experience with Lancôme Trésor

I approached Trésor with curiosity about this long-standing fragrance from Lancôme's prestige line. Released in the 1990s, it represents a particular style of floral perfumery that dominated that era—romantic, unabashedly feminine, and built around classic rose accords.

### The Scent Profile

The fragrance opens with a combination of rose, apricot blossom, and peach notes that immediately signal its romantic intentions. There's a softness to the opening that feels deliberate, avoiding sharp citrus or green notes in favor of rounded fruit-floral tones. As it develops, the heart reveals more traditional floral elements including lily and iris, creating a bouquet effect that feels cohesive rather than jumbled.

The base includes vanilla, sandalwood, and musk, which provide warmth without becoming heavy or cloying. The overall composition is smooth and well-blended—I never experienced harsh transitions between notes. However, the formula does feel firmly rooted in its era's aesthetic. If you're looking for modern twists, unconventional accords, or edgy elements, this isn't that fragrance.

### Performance Characteristics

In terms of longevity, I found Trésor lasted around 6-8 hours on my skin before fading to a skin scent. The projection was moderate—noticeable in the first 2-3 hours within arm's length, then becoming more intimate. This is reasonable for an eau de parfum, though I've worn other fragrances at this concentration that performed more powerfully.

The sillage is polite rather than commanding. It won't fill a room or leave a strong trail, which some will appreciate as considerate and others might find underwhelming given the price point.

### Practical Considerations

This is decidedly a special-occasion fragrance for me. The romantic, traditional character makes it feel appropriate for formal events, dinner dates, or occasions where you want to project classic femininity. I found it less suitable for everyday office wear or casual weekend activities—it has a dressed-up quality that can feel mismatched with jeans and a t-shirt.

The scent also skews more mature in feel. While fragrance preferences are personal, Trésor's composition aligns with traditional luxury perfume expectations rather than contemporary trends toward fresh, minimalist, or unconventional scents.

### Final Thoughts

Trésor is a competent, well-made fragrance that delivers exactly what it promises: a romantic floral experience in the classic French perfume tradition. The quality is evident in the smooth blending and refined materials. However, at its price point, I expected either more distinctive character or notably better performance. It's a safe choice if this style appeals to you, but it doesn't offer much that would make me choose it over other options in the same category. For those who specifically love traditional floral bouquet perfumes and appreciate Lancôme's heritage, it's worth sampling. For anyone seeking something more versatile or contemporary, I'd suggest exploring alternatives.
