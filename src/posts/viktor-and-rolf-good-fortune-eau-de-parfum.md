## My Experience with Viktor & Rolf Good Fortune

I approached this fragrance with curiosity, given Viktor & Rolf's reputation for bold, memorable scents. Good Fortune positions itself as a modern floral-gourmand composition, and from my first spray, the sweet character announces itself clearly. This isn't a subtle fragrance—it makes a statement from the opening.

The scent profile leans decidedly sweet, with noticeable warmth that develops over the first thirty minutes of wear. I detected floral elements that provide some structure, but the overall impression remains firmly in gourmand territory. For those who appreciate fragrances like Flowerbomb or similar sweet offerings, the DNA will feel familiar, though Good Fortune carves out its own identity within that family.

What struck me most was the evolution—or relative lack thereof. The fragrance maintains a fairly consistent character from opening to drydown, which can be seen as either reliability or predictability depending on your perspective. I found myself appreciating the coherence, though I would have welcomed more complexity in the heart notes.

Regarding performance, my experience showed moderate longevity. I typically got six to seven hours of noticeable wear before it became a skin scent. The initial projection is strong enough to garner compliments in the first two hours, then it settles into a more intimate radius. This is adequate for most purposes, though those seeking all-day performance without reapplication may be disappointed.

The versatility presents some limitations. I found this works best in cooler months and for evening occasions. When I wore it during warmer days or in office settings, the sweetness felt heavy and somewhat out of place. It's distinctly a "going out" fragrance rather than a daily signature scent. This narrower use case affects the overall value proposition.

The bottle design maintains Viktor & Rolf's artistic sensibility, and the presentation feels appropriately premium. The visual appeal shouldn't be underestimated—it's the kind of bottle that looks intentional on a vanity.

For someone specifically seeking a sweet, statement-making evening fragrance and who already enjoys gourmand compositions, Good Fortune delivers a competent option. The quality feels consistent with the designer category, and the scent itself is well-constructed within its chosen lane.

However, I'd recommend sampling before purchasing. The sweetness is pronounced, and what reads as comforting warmth to some may feel excessive to others. The moderate longevity and specific wearability also mean you should consider how often you'd realistically reach for this in your rotation.

Good Fortune succeeds at what it sets out to do—deliver a bold, sweet, celebratory fragrance. Whether that aligns with your needs depends entirely on your preference for gourmand scents and how much versatility you require from your fragrance wardrobe.
