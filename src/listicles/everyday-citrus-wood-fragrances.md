The citrus-and-woods pairing is perfumery's most reliable formula for everyday wear: fresh enough for morning meetings, substantive enough to last through dinner. These fragrances sidestep the polarizing sweetness of gourmands and the density of pure orientals, instead offering transparency married to structure. What distinguishes them is how each handles the transition from top to base—whether the citrus fades gracefully or the woods arrive too abruptly.
