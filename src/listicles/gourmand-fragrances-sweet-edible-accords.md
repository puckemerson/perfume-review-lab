Gourmand fragrances translate edible notes into wearable scent, pulling from pastry counters and spice cabinets rather than flower gardens. The category can tip into saccharine territory, but the strongest examples balance sweetness with structure—pairing vanilla with tobacco, tempering caramel with salt, or anchoring praline with woods. These picks showcase gourmand composition at its most deliberate.
