Eau de toilette often gets dismissed as the weaker option, but concentration isn't everything. These EDT formulations offer distinct advantages: better projection in warm weather, softer sillage for close-quarter settings, and compositions that actually benefit from lighter dilution. Each of these earns its place not despite being eau de toilette, but because of it.
